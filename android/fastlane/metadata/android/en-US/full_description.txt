StickerPants is the outfit tracker that turns your fits into WhatsApp stickers — with a playful roast on every look. Log what you wear, when you wore it, and get on with your day.

CUTOUTS BUILT FOR CLOTHES, NOT JUST PEOPLE
• Three segmentation engines run on your phone — the person model goes first, then a garment-aware model takes over
• Hair, skin, clothes and accessories are recognised separately, so a jacket on a hanger or jeans folded on a bed cuts out clean
• Cut out a whole outfit or a single piece — not just full-body shots
• Automatic fallback if an engine can't read the photo, so you still get a cutout
• Everything runs on-device, so your pictures are never uploaded anywhere

LOG YOUR OUTFITS THE EASY WAY
• Snap a photo and log the fit in seconds — just log outfits with a photo, like you always meant to
• Every sticker is stamped with its date, so you always know when you wore it
• Scroll back through your outfit history and see what you actually reach for
• Answer "what should I wear?" by checking what already worked

YOUR OUTFIT CALENDAR AT A GLANCE
• Build a digital closet log that records what you actually wear, not just what you own
• A dated timeline of every fit you've logged, ready to scroll back through
• Rewear your favorites with pride — the repeats are the point
• Get more wear out of the clothes you already have instead of buying more
• Your wardrobe log stays private and on your phone

GET ROASTED (CARROT WEATHER STYLE)
• Every sticker earns a witty one-liner about the fit, in the spirit of Carrot Weather's forecasts
• The roast is always about the fit, never about you — no body talk, ever
• Tap refresh for a new line, and keep the ones worth framing

SHARE YOUR STICKERS
• Turn your cutout into a WhatsApp sticker with square silhouettes and expressive shapes
• Shapes and colours that look right in a chat window
• Save sticker packs and send your fits straight to WhatsApp

YOUR FIT, ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
• Two homescreen widgets: your latest sticker, or a grid of your recent looks
• Widgets rotate themselves every few hours, so your fits keep changing all day
• Each sticker keeps its own shape and colour, so the widget matches your gallery

STYLE IT YOUR WAY
• Your own style, on your terms — no feed, no likes, no outfit ranking
• A private outfit history that works without an account

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your photos never leave your device for cutouts. No account, no feed, no cloud — just you and your wardrobe.
